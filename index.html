❓ How to pull off the perfect forgery

  • 🧴 Squeeze paint tubes into the mixing dish. Mix them like real paint: red + yellow = orange, add white to lighten, black to darken.
  • ✨ When your mix matches a color in the masterpiece, you'll see “It's in the painting!”
  • 🖌️ Paint on the easel by dragging on it, or pick the 🪣 bucket to fill a whole pencil-sketched shape at once.
  • 🔍 Tap the little reference picture to get the recipe for any color.
  • 🔦 When Bernard the guard turns around, FREEZE! Don't paint while his flashlight is on you (mixing is fine). Three close calls and Bernard spots you (no harm done, just try again).
  • 🔄 When it looks right, press SWAP IT! The closer the likeness, the more stars and francs you earn.
  • 🎨 You start up close at the Easel. Switch to Room to watch Bernard, or Peek to study the original. (Keys 1, 2 and 3 do the same.)
